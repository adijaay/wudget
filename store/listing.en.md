## Google Play

**Title (30 char)**
wudget: Expense Tracker

**Short description (80 char)**
Track spending and budgets, no account needed. Your data stays on your phone.

**Full description (4000 char)**

wudget helps you track what you spend and earn, no account, no sign-up, no
internet connection required.

**Log it in seconds**
Open the app, pick a wallet, type the amount, save. A built-in calculator adds
up a receipt right on the entry screen.

**Wallets, not just a balance**
Track cash, bank accounts, e-wallets, credit cards, savings, and debts as
separate wallets. A credit card payment is recorded as a transfer automatically,
never double-counted as an expense.

**See your pace, not just a total**
This period's budget is compared against how fast you're actually spending
today, so you know whether you're on track before the month ends, not after.

**A budget to start from**
Never made a budget before? wudget can propose a starting budget based on your
own recent spending, which you can adjust anytime.

**Recurring bills, remembered**
Set up a monthly bill once, and wudget reminds you and helps you log it when it
comes due.

**Coming from another app**
Already have records elsewhere? Import them from a CSV file instead of typing
everything in again.

**Your data is yours**
No account, no phone number, no email required. Everything is stored on your
own phone. Back it up anytime, and restore it whenever you need to.

wudget is built for anyone who wants to start tracking money without a sign-up
process getting in the way first.
