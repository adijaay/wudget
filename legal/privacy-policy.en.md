# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [fill in before publishing]

wudget is a personal finance tracker built to work entirely on your device. This
policy explains what that means in practice.

## Data we collect

None. wudget does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. There
is no account, no email address, no phone number, and no sign-in of any kind.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter, wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring bills,
is stored in a local database on your device only. It never leaves your device
unless you export it yourself (see "Backups and exports" below).

## Permissions the app requests, and why

- **Camera**, when you choose to attach a photo to a transaction. The photo is
  saved to the app's local storage on your device; it is never uploaded anywhere.
- **Notifications**, to remind you about upcoming bills you have scheduled. These
  reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device.
- **Files**, when you choose to import a CSV file from another app, or to export
  or restore a backup. The app only reads or writes the specific file you pick.

## Backups and exports

wudget lets you create a backup file (JSON) or export your data (CSV/JSON) and
save it wherever you choose, including a location synced by another app (such as
your device's built-in cloud backup, if you have that turned on). wudget itself
does not upload this file anywhere. Anything that happens to it after you save it
is governed by that other app or service's own privacy policy, not this one.

## Analytics

wudget keeps a small local log of anonymous, on-device usage events (for example,
which of two equivalent layouts you were shown for a screen) so the app's own
design can be evaluated. This log never leaves your device, is never transmitted
anywhere, and contains no identifying information.

## Third parties

wudget does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any
data to share. wudget contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or
tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

wudget does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If wudget's data handling changes in a future version, for example if optional
cloud sync is added, this policy will be updated first, and the update will be
clearly dated.
